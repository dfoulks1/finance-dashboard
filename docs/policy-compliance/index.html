Policy Compliance¶

This project follows the ASF Generative Tooling Guidance. The full obligations are in AI Contribution Guide §3; this page is the fast checklist. Read the guide — this is a reminder, not a substitute.

Every AI-assisted contribution must¶

  • [ ] Have a human contributor who read and understood the output and can defend it in review. Prompts do not create authorship.
  • [ ] Satisfy your ICLA obligations — you are entitled to grant the license (ICLA §4), the work is your original creation / third-party material is disclosed (§5), and you will notify legal-private@apache.org if a representation later proves inaccurate (§8). Do not route AI output through §7.
  • [ ] Meet Condition 1 — the AI tool's terms of use grant you sufficient rights in the output and impose no restrictions inconsistent with the Open Source Definition.
  • [ ] Meet Condition 2 — at least one of:
    • 2.1 output is not copyrightable (legally unsettled — don't rely on alone)
    • 2.2 no third-party material (preferred)
    • 2.3 third-party material is compatibly licensed and compliant (preferred)
  • [ ] Disclose provenance in the commit trailer: Generated-by: <Tool> <Version> or Co-authored-by: <Tool> <Version>.
  • [ ] License-check dependencies against the 3rd Party Licensing Policy and record them in the SBOM.
  • [ ] Human-review before public publication (EU AI Act): this docs site and any announcement/release note must be reviewed by a committer/PMC member before it publishes, or carry a clear AI-generated label.

How this repo satisfies the publishing rule¶

The docs site is built locally from the default branch after human-reviewed merges — source in docs-src/, built HTML committed to docs/ and served by GitHub Pages (/docs, no CI). The reviewer who merges takes responsibility for the published text, which is exactly what the EU AI Act path requires — no extra labeling is needed as long as the review gate holds. If content is ever auto-published without review, it must be labeled AI-generated.

Tooling¶

The ASF maintains no approved-tools list — use any tool that lets you meet the above. Don't second-guess a vendor's terms of use; your usage is bound by the totality of their ToU.
